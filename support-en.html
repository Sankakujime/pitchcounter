PitchKeeper is a pitch counter for youth baseball scorekeepers. Tap +1 for every pitch, track both teams inning by inning, and see each pitcher's daily limit and required rest days.

FAQ

Which pitch count rules does the app use?

By default, the app uses "Age-based + rest days": a daily pitch limit by age, plus required rest days based on how many pitches were thrown. The numbers follow the MLB and USA Baseball Pitch Smart guidelines (as of 2026). It also warns you when a pitcher would pitch on three consecutive days or has already pitched in another game that day.

Leagues and tournaments may have their own rules. Always check your league's current rules.

How do I set my players' ages?

Tap the settings button (the slider icon) at the top of the Count screen and set the Team age. To set an age for an individual player, open Pitchers, swipe left on the player, and tap Edit. Players without an age use the Team age.

Can I use a per-game limit instead?

Yes. In the settings (Rules), switch the rule to "Per-game limit" and set the limit and warning lines yourself.

What happens to the count when the date changes?

The Count screen shows only today's pitches, so it starts again from 0 the next day. Past records are kept and can be viewed in the History tab. They are also used to calculate rest days.

Can I count the opponent's pitches too?

Yes. Switch between "Our team" and "Opponent" at the top of the Count screen. In the Check tab, you can compare the counts inning by inning with the other team's scorekeeper.

The count doesn't appear on the Lock Screen

Make sure "Show count on Lock Screen" is on in the settings, and that Live Activities are allowed for PitchKeeper in the iPhone Settings app. The count appears after you tap +1.

How do I see the guide again?

Open the settings and tap "How to Use".

How do I delete records?

In Pitchers, swipe left on a player. A player with pitch records is first hidden (Hide). To delete them completely, open "Hidden players", swipe left on the player, and tap Delete. This also deletes all of that player's pitch records. Uninstalling the app erases all data stored on your device.
