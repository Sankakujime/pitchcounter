Privacy Policy

App: PitchKeeper  ·  日本語

PitchKeeper ("the App") is a pitch counting app for youth baseball scorekeepers. This policy explains how the App handles information.

Information We Collect

The App does not collect any personal information. It does not communicate with any server and works without an internet connection.

The following information you enter in the App is stored only on your device and is never sent to the developer or any third party:

  • Player names, jersey numbers, and ages (optional)
  • Pitch records (date, time, and inning)
  • Game names and opponent names
  • App settings, such as the pitch count rules and team age

Sharing

When you export an image, text, or CSV file with the App's Share feature, you choose where to send it (Messages, email, Files, etc.) using your device's share sheet. The App itself never sends this content to the developer or any third party.

Lock Screen

If you turn on "Show count on Lock Screen", the current pitcher's name and pitch count are shown on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. This display is created on your device and is not sent anywhere.

Ads, Analytics, and Tracking

The App does not show ads, does not use analytics, and does not track you in any way.

Deleting Your Data

To delete your data, use the delete features in the App or uninstall the App. Uninstalling the App erases all data stored on your device.

Children's Privacy

The App is meant to be used by adult scorekeepers, who enter players' names, jersey numbers, and ages. This information is stored only on the device and is never sent off the device. The App does not collect any information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the App adds new features. Any changes will be posted on this page.

For questions about the App, please see the Support page.
